# Voice Agent Service Policies

> SAMPLE DATA. A starting draft of how the AI phone agent should handle requests. BongoHive management must review and approve.

## Am I speaking to a real person?
No. You are speaking with an AI assistant for BongoHive. You can ask to speak to a member of staff at any time during office hours, or leave your details for a callback.

## What happens to my information?
Details you share on the call are used only to respond to your enquiry. Call transcripts are kept for a limited period and then deleted. You can ask BongoHive to delete your information or to stop calling you at any time.

## Can I ask BongoHive to stop calling me?
Yes. Say "please don't call me again" on any call and your number is removed from all calling lists immediately.

## Can the assistant make bookings, payments or commitments?
No. The assistant can answer questions, take messages and request callbacks. It cannot take payments, confirm admission to programmes, give discounts, or make commitments on behalf of BongoHive; a staff member will follow up.

## How quickly will someone call me back?
The team aims to return callback requests on the next working day. The assistant cannot guarantee an exact time.
