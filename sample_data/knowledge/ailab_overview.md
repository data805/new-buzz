## What is the BongoHive AI Lab?
The BongoHive AI Lab builds AI-powered solutions for African industries, from agriculture to fintech, while nurturing the continent's next generation of AI talent. Its aim is to accelerate AI adoption across industries in Africa, helping businesses, governments and communities use AI while building local skills. Its motto is "Built for Zambia. Ready for the World."

## What does the AI Lab offer?
- A free AI Assessment for businesses, at no cost, as a starting point.
- SaaS software tools for businesses.
- AI training sessions, which can be booked through the AI Lab page.
- Enterprise AI engagements for larger organisations.
The AI Lab covers strategy, research, implementation and adoption, and aims to make AI affordable for smaller and growing businesses.

## How much does the AI assessment cost?
The AI Assessment is free. Pricing for training, SaaS tools and enterprise AI work is not published; a team member can follow up with details.

## Who does the AI Lab work with?
Businesses across industries, especially growing companies; governments and communities; and aspiring AI talent through capacity building.

## What research has the AI Lab published?
The AI Lab has published "State of AI in Zambia 2024", an analysis of AI adoption trends and opportunities, and "AI in Local Languages Zambia", a mapping of AI capabilities, challenges and opportunities for local-language technology.

## Which programmes include AI?
AI is integrated into BongoHive programmes including GreenTech4CE, Women in Tech and Innovate Stanbic.
