# About BongoHive

> SAMPLE DATA. Summarised from the public website https://bongohive.co.zm (retrieved 27 October 2026). BongoHive staff must verify and approve before production use.

## What is BongoHive?
BongoHive describes itself as Zambia's leading innovation hub. Its tagline is "Technology. Entrepreneurship. Innovation." BongoHive works through innovation, entrepreneurship and technology to co-create solutions that drive sustainable growth across the continent. BongoHive has been operating since 2011, which made 2026 its fifteenth year.

## Where is BongoHive located?
BongoHive is at ZEP-RE Business Park, Mass Media, Alick Nkhata Road, Lusaka 10101, Zambia.

## How can I contact BongoHive?
The phone number published on the BongoHive coworking page is +260 211 845 600. You can also use the contact form at bongohive.co.zm/get-in-touch. BongoHive is on YouTube, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Facebook and Instagram.

## What does BongoHive offer?
BongoHive describes "one organisation, five integrated capabilities":
- Tech Consulting: end-to-end product development, from discovery and design to deployment and support.
- Innovation Consulting: turning strategy into implementation through innovation, digital transformation and technology advisory.
- Coworking and Workspace: a professional workspace inside Zambia's most connected innovation ecosystem.
- Data and AI: AI solutions designed for African business realities, delivered by the BongoHive AI Lab.
- Entrepreneurship Support: structured programmes, mentorship and direct investor access for first-time founders and established businesses.

## What impact has BongoHive had?
The BongoHive website lists more than 200 startups supported and more than one million US dollars of funding raised by alumni.

## Has BongoHive worked with Zamtel?
Yes. The website describes a Zamtel digital transformation case study in which BongoHive partnered with Zamtel to design and implement a digital transformation strategy, covering customer journey mapping, process automation and staff capability building. Specific results figures are not published.

## Does BongoHive operate outside Zambia?
Yes. Since COVID-19 BongoHive operations outside Zambia are mainly remote, with travel arranged on a case-by-case basis.
