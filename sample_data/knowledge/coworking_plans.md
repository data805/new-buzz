# Hive Coworking at BongoHive

> SAMPLE DATA. Summarised from https://bongohive.co.zm/coworking/ (retrieved 27 October 2026). Prices are NOT published; never quote a price. Verify before production use.

## What coworking plans are available?
- Hot Desk: for freelancers, consultants and remote workers. Access Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm. Includes free scanning, black and white printing at 3 kwacha per page, high-speed internet and community access.
- Hot Desk Plus: for professionals who need more flexibility. Please confirm access hours with the team when enquiring.
- Hot Desk Premium: monthly plan only. Includes 24/7 building access, phone booths, 3 meeting room hours per month, free scanning and black and white printing at 3 kwacha per page.
- Private Office: for corporate teams, project units and growing startups. Includes a fully dedicated space, 24/7 building access, 8 meeting room hours per month, phone booths and 30 free black and white prints per month.
- Virtual Office: a business address without a desk. Includes a business address and mail handling, a dedicated phone line, on-demand meeting room access and access to the BongoHive ecosystem.

## How much does coworking cost?
Prices are not published. Every plan says "enquire for pricing". The agent should offer to take the caller's details so the coworking team can share current pricing.

## What amenities are included?
High-speed fibre internet of 100 Mbps or more, free on-site compound parking with no time limits, AV-equipped meeting rooms and boardrooms, event space with event hosting and catering options, on-site support staff, networking events, and access to workshops and mentorship.

## Can I visit or try the space?
Yes. You can book a free day pass or book a tour through the links on the coworking page of the BongoHive website.

## What are the coworking hours?
Hot Desk access is Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm. Hot Desk Premium and Private Office members have 24/7 building access.
