# BongoHive Programmes

> SAMPLE DATA. Summarised from https://bongohive.co.zm (retrieved 27 October 2026). Programme dates change; staff must keep this page current.

## What programmes does BongoHive run?
BongoHive offers incubation, acceleration, mentorship and investor access for founders, plus programmes such as GreenTech4CE, Women in Tech and Innovate Stanbic. The current list is at bongohive.co.zm/programmes.

## What is the AI for Business Accelerator 2026?
A 12-week intensive programme for African startups building AI-powered solutions. Benefits include technical mentorship, business advisory, corporate pilot partnerships and up to 50,000 US dollars in follow-on funding.
