# BongoHive Frequently Asked Questions

> SAMPLE DATA. Paraphrased from https://bongohive.co.zm/faq/ (retrieved 27 October 2026). Verify before production use.

## How does BongoHive define innovation?
Innovation means improving an existing product or service by finding gaps and applying targeted solutions.

## What is the BongoHive Innovation Readiness Framework?
A survey and assessment tool that measures an organisation's internal innovation culture, mindset and readiness to adopt new solutions.

## How are ideas chosen and prioritised?
Ideas are ranked by feasibility, impact, viability and urgency.

## What training is available for innovation teams, startups or corporates?
BongoHive offers a 7-week hybrid Applied Human-Centred Design course for corporate innovation teams and practitioners.

## What is the Idea Spring platform?
Idea Spring is BongoHive's in-house tool that helps innovation managers collect, score and rate ideas for internal and external challenges.

## Can BongoHive help with prototyping?
Yes. BongoHive is strongest in digital prototypes and process mapping, and also trains staff to build prototypes themselves.

## How can I stay connected with the BongoHive innovation community?
Through bimonthly peer events, webinars and ask-me-anything sessions, networking events, and BongoHive's two newsletters.

## Does BongoHive host student tours?
Yes. Student tours run on the last Friday of each month and can include talks, showcases and workspace visits. Universities, colleges, lecturers, student groups and academic departments can request a tour, usually through an institutional representative.

## Does BongoHive partner with universities?
Yes, on programmes covering entrepreneurship, incubation, research commercialisation, digital and AI skills, hackathons, student challenges and industry projects. BongoHive can also help design or strengthen university innovation hubs and entrepreneurship centres. A university can start by contacting BongoHive with the type of engagement it wants.

## Can students pitch ideas to BongoHive?
Depending on the engagement, students may pitch, receive feedback, join challenges, or be connected to programmes.

## What advice does BongoHive give organisations starting innovation work?
Start small and build internal champions in a small team. Focus on three pillars: people (culture and skills), process (workflows that allow cheap, early failure, such as five-day design sprints) and product (digital tools and prototyping).
